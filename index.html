The Doggo Guide

🍗

Snackos & Chomp

Dogs will stare at you while you eat. Do not fall for the puppy eyes! Give them healthy kibble twice a day.

Pro tip: Chimken is life, but chocolate is a BIG NO-NO. 🛑

🎾

NO TAKE! Only Throw.

Doggos need zoomies. Take them for walks so they don't eat your favorite shoes. Remember the golden rule of fetch: They will bring the ball back, but they will not let it go.

💩

Potty Business

If they start sniffing the carpet like a detective, RUN! Take them outside immediately. When they do business outside, throw a massive party with treats. 🎉

🐈

The Kitty Guide

🐟

3 AM Tuna Demands

Cats can see the bottom of the bowl and will assume they are starving. Mix wet food and dry food. Fresh water is a must, preferably placed in a spot they can awkwardly drink from.

📦

The Box Rule

Do not buy expensive toys. Buy a $100 item just to give them the cardboard box it came in. Laser pointers are magic, but never use your hands as toys unless you want scratches!

🏜️

The Sand Box

Cats are neat freaks. Give them a clean litter box (scoop daily!). If it's dirty, they might choose your laundry basket instead. You are now a professional poop scooper. Congrats!
